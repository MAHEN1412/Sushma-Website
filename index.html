About & Craft

Tool stack

I start most projects the same way a researcher would — with a question I can't yet answer, and a whiteboard of assumptions to break. But I finish them the way a graphic designer does: obsessing over the kerning on a button label, the weight of a rule, the one photograph that sets the whole tone.

That combination is the point. A flow can be usable and still feel like nobody cared how it looked; a brand can be beautiful and still confuse the person trying to check out. Ten years across non-profits, logistics, and fintech have taught me the two aren't separate disciplines — they're one craft, practiced at different zoom levels.

"Good UX gets out of the way. Good design makes you want to stay a little longer."
